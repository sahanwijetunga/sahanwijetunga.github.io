Hello! I am a first year pure math undergrad at UCLA, having switched over from computer science in Winter 2025. I'm broadly interested in analysis, algebraic number theory, and formalization. 

I'm currently learning some additive combinatorics (through [Tao & Vu's book](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/additive-combinatorics/D408BA34B567974CC8FB0CEC2A49A807)) and finishing up my REU at Tufts. I plan to learn some PDEs in the Fall, and clean up my formalization results from the REU, to contribute to mathlib. I'm also looking into contributing to the [FLT project](https://github.com/ImperialCollegeLondon/flt) and potentially some analysis oriented formalization efforts.
